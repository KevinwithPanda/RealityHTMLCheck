ONE CONSERVATIVE DELIVERY ANSWER

Know why the release is blocked.

release-decision selects the newest valid quality gate, verification, policy, trust, risk, and repair-review artifacts; enforces required controls and evidence age; binds each selected source by SHA-256; and returns GO, REVIEW, or NO-GO. It records a decision but never deploys or approves a release.
