Results and next steps

Online ZIP repair is a safe-metadata working copy, not a publish or live-deployment verdict. Browser repair stays limited to doctype, language, and early UTF-8 metadata. The Skill may make broader justified edits only inside a separate copy.

What happens after the check? The no-clone Codex Skill path returns the before technical report, a repaired working copy, the after report, and detected unresolved decisions—without overwriting the originals. It labels a copy directly usable only after that verification meets the delivery gate. For deployment, use the v0.13 local command or Action to build a verified capsule and bind the live HTTPS URL to its exact bytes. See publish and live proof →

WHAT IT CHECKS

Four practical questions

1

Is the content complete?Encoding, empty exports, unfinished AI placeholders.

2

Will the folder still work elsewhere?Missing files, broken links, machine-only and case-sensitive paths.

3

Is it easy to read?Headings, contents links, tables, images, and phone layouts.

4

Are known execution or sharing risks present?Executable scripts, insecure resources, and remote dependencies. Static checks are not a security certification.
